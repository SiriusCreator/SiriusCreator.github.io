About the App

Passport Photo Maker allows you to quickly crop, format, and prepare official photos according to passport, visa, and identification requirements for various countries. With automatic/manual background removal, precise cropping tools, and print layouts (such as standard photo sheet sizes), preparing your official documents has never been easier.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I select the right size for my passport or visa? Select your target country or document type from the predefined preset list, or manually enter custom millimeter/inch dimensions required by your local agency.

Can I change the background color? Yes, you can automatically or manually remove the original background and replace it with standard white, light grey, or custom plain colors required by document guidelines.

How do I print my photos? You can save the formatted photo sheet (e.g., 4x6", 5x7", or A4 layout) directly to your device gallery and print it at home or at any local photo center.

What if I encounter an issue? Check our troubleshooting section below or contact us at siriuscreator999@gmail.com.

Troubleshooting

App Crashes / Freeze: Ensure your device is running the latest operating system updates. Clearing the app cache or reinstalling can resolve temporary glitches.

Background Removal Issues: For optimal automatic background removal results, take your photo against a high-contrast background with good lighting.

Export/Save Errors: Ensure the app has permission to access your device’s storage or photo gallery to save high-resolution output files.
