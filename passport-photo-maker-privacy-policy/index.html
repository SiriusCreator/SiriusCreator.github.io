Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we collect minimal data required to operate the app. We do not require or collect your name, email address, or phone number:

1. Anonymous Authentication

Our app utilizes anonymous login functionality. When you use the app, an anonymous unique identifier (UID) is automatically generated to manage your session and save your app preferences. We do not collect, request, or store your name, email address, or personal profile details.

2. Image Processing & Camera Access

The core functionality of Passport Photo Maker requires access to your device's camera and photo gallery.

  • Offline Face Detection: We use your photos for face detection offline locally using Google ML Kit. We do not use your photos for facial recognition, AI training, or any purpose other than rendering the background removal service you requested.
  • Third-Party Processing: To provide our background removal feature, the specific photos you choose to edit are transmitted via API to a third-party service provider (remove.bg). Please note that these images are processed by remove.bg according to their own privacy standards and data retention policies.

3. In-App Purchases, Subscriptions & RevenueCat

We offer premium features through optional purchase plans, which include short-term passes (3-Day Access), recurring auto-renewable plans (Yearly Subscription), and non-consumable one-time unlocks (Lifetime Access).

All payment transactions and billing are handled directly and securely by the respective app stores (Apple App Store or Google Play Store). We do not collect, process, or store your credit card details, banking credentials, or billing addresses on our servers.

To manage in-app purchases, validate store receipts, grant feature entitlements, and handle subscription states seamlessly across your devices, we utilize a third-party service called RevenueCat. RevenueCat processes anonymous device identifiers (App User IDs), purchase timestamps, transaction receipts, product identifiers, and active entitlement statuses. No personal identifiers (such as your actual name or email address) are shared with or processed by RevenueCat.

Managing and Canceling Subscriptions: Auto-renewable subscriptions (such as 3-Day or Yearly plans) automatically renew unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. You can manage, view, or cancel your active subscriptions at any time through your device's account settings (Apple ID Settings or Google Play Store Subscriptions).
